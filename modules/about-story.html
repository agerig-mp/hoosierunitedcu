Our Story

Shaped by community

Hoosier United Credit Union began as Farm Bureau Employees Credit Union and expanded over the decades through mission-aligned mergers that broadened who we serve while keeping the member-first model intact.

In the 1980s, the credit union became Hoosier Farm Bureau Credit Union after a merger that allowed us to serve more people.

In March 2013, we merged with IKIBEW Federal Credit Union, welcoming IBEW Local 481 members into the HUCU family. In July 2022, we merged with SMW Federal Credit Union, bringing SMART Local 20 members into that same circle. That growth matters because it reflects the way HUCU has always operated: locally rooted, relationship-driven, and committed to helping members understand credit, save wisely, and make financial progress with real people at their side.
