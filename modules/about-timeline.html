Milestones

Key moments in the HUCU story

Our history is a steady expansion of access, not a move away from personal service.

1931

Farm Bureau Employees Credit Union is chartered

HUCU became a state chartered credit union on May 26, 1931, serving Indiana Farm Bureau employees.

1980s

Broader membership through merger growth

The organization became Hoosier Farm Bureau Credit Union, expanding its reach while preserving its community-first approach.

2013

IBEW Local 481 joins the HUCU family

The merger with IKIBEW Federal Credit Union brought International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers members into our membership base.

2022

SMART Local 20 members are welcomed in

The SMW Federal Credit Union merger added SMART Local 20 members and strengthened HUCU’s Indianapolis union footprint.
