# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Credit union members and prospective members visiting a public-facing website, often looking for a branch, phone number, or a quick way to ask for help.

## Product Purpose

Help members confidently find the right Hoosier United Credit Union contact path, location, and next step.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, straightforward. The experience should feel calm and useful, especially when a member needs help quickly.

## Anti-references

Avoid long informational pages, vague contact routing, dense institutional copy, and decorative interface patterns that slow down the member's next action.

## Design Principles

- Make the next contact action obvious.
- Keep branch-specific information easy to scan.
- Use plain language and a reassuring tone.
- Preserve clear navigation across mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic structure, clear labels, keyboard-accessible controls, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion-friendly behavior.
